# Solving Conflict

I think it's normal to have conflicts and misunderstandings because we're all human, whether we're team members or leaders, but members and leaders are also part of the same team.
But since team members and leaders belong to the same team, it's not about who's right or who wins.
But, in the end, it's not about who's right or who wins, but about "solving the team's problems" or "achieving the team's goals," so you have to put your head down and communicate,
Through communication, we can deeply understand each other's problems and thoughts, and once we understand each other, we can analyze the issues that we are facing.
Once we understand each other, we analyze the issues we are facing, decide which of them should be prioritized, and find the best way to deal with them together.

For the most recent project, we decided to use the DDD architecture, but we had not yet decided on how to write the code and build the folder.
The leader and I discussed how to do it together, and there were still many points of conflict, so what I wanted to do was to use a standardized way to separate layers,
I wanted to use the standard way of writing when an event was triggered, but it took a lot of time and was difficult for the other members.
I was working on a project with a tight deadline, and the leader's way was not as standard as my way, but it didn't take as long and the other members could figure it out quickly.
So we went with the leader's way because it took less time and was quicker to grasp.

So I think communication is very important to resolve conflicts and contradictions.
