My strengths lie in decisiveness and perseverance. When I set out to do something, despite encountering numerous difficulties along the way, I always persist until the end. Even if the outcome is not perfect, I believe that at least I dared to try, challenged myself, and despite the results not meeting expectations, I trust that I will learn something.

Furthermore, before undertaking any task, whether large or small, I meticulously plan each step before taking action. Specifically, I consider what I will do at each stage, what preparations are needed, and what potential issues may arise.

An example of this is the job-hopping interview, for instance. Six months prior, I planned when I would transition jobs and what I needed to do beforehand. Specifically, starting from October, I reviewed the knowledge I had gained from my current job, such as infrastructure, AWS, and microservices. In early November, I began focusing on live coding practice by honing my skills on platforms like LeetCode. Concurrently, I engaged with recruitment agents to learn more about the companies I would be interviewing with.

My weakness is that I tend to overthink, sometimes making simple issues more complex than necessary. I am an introverted person, often keeping my true personality hidden, so when I come across an unusual look or gesture, I am usually quite curious about why the other person did that to me. As I mentioned, overthinking can complicate matters and sometimes significantly slow down my actions.

I had a memorable experience in August this year when I traveled alone to Aoshima Island in Miyazaki. The scenery was truly beautiful, but after enjoying the view and preparing to return to the hotel, it started raining heavily. I couldn't catch a bus or taxi, and there were no houses or people around on the road, with mountains on one side and the sea on the other. I was really worried, and thoughts like "it's over," "everything has come to an end," "there's no way to salvage this" crossed my mind.

However, suddenly, I realized that I needed to stay calm, keep walking, and luckily, after walking in the rain for about 30 minutes, I found a family who kindly gave me a ride back to the hotel in their car. Through this experience, I realized that thinking without taking action is meaningless. Just take action, even if you fail, you will learn from it instead of doing nothing. Moreover, thinking without action or without leading to any result is entirely pointless. Therefore, to overcome this weakness, I will focus more on taking action as quickly as possible instead of overthinking.
