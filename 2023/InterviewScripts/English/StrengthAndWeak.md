# Strength And Weak

My strengths include determination and perseverance. When I want to accomplish something, no matter how hard it is, I will do it to the end. Even if the result is not perfect, I have the courage to at least try, challenge myself, and believe that I can learn something even if it is not what I wanted.

For example, when I solved an issue for your company, I was told that I could solve it in a few hours when I originally received the assignment from the agent, but I was still not finished even after 5 hours, and the main reason was that I applied DDD architecture and wrote unit tests properly. The main reason was that I had to apply the DDD architecture and write the unit test properly.

Also, before doing anything, no matter how big or small, I always have a plan in place. Specifically, I think about what I will do at each stage, what preparations are needed, and what risks may arise.

As an example, let's say I'm changing jobs. 3 months ago I planned when I would change jobs and what I would do before that. Specifically, in October I started reflecting on the knowledge I had learned so far in my current position; in early November I started practicing practical coding on platforms such as LeetCode. At the same time, I worked with a recruitment agent to find companies to interview with.

My weakness is that I think too often and sometimes make simple problems more complicated than necessary.

In fact, in August of this year, I traveled alone to Aoshima Island in Miyazaki, Japan. It was really beautiful, and after enjoying the scenery, I was about to return to my hotel when suddenly it started raining heavily. I couldn't take a bus or a cab, there were no houses or people on the road, mountains on one side and the sea on the other. I was really in despair, thinking, "It's over," "Everything is over," and "There is no way to save this.

But suddenly I realized that I needed to stay calm and keep walking. Luckily, after about 20 minutes of walking in the rain, I met a kind family and they drove me to my hotel. Through this experience, I realized that just thinking and not taking action is meaningless and without consequence, and that in the end the idea is completely meaningless. Therefore, in order to overcome this weakness, from now on we must take action as soon as possible instead of thinking excessively.
