# Strength And Weak

My strengths include determination and perseverance. When I want to accomplish something, no matter how hard it is, I will do it to the end. Even if the result is not perfect, I have the courage to at least try, challenge myself, and believe that I can learn something from it.

For example, when I solved an issue for your company, I was told that I could solve it in one day but I was still not finished even after 1 day, and the main reason was that I applied DDD architecture and wrote unit tests properly. Because I want to make my answer to be the best as I can

Also, before doing anything, no matter how big or small, I always have a plan. Specifically, I think about what I will do at each stage, what preparations are needed, and what risks may arise.

As an example, let's say I'm changing jobs. 3 months ago I planned when I would change jobs and what I would do before that. Specifically, in October I started reflecting on the knowledge I had learned and in early November I started practicing practical coding on LeetCode. At the same time, I worked with a recruitment agent to find companies to interview with.

My weakness is that I think too often and sometimes make simple problems more complicated than necessary.

In fact, in August of this year, I traveled alone to Aoshima Island in Miyazaki, Japan. It was really beautiful, and after enjoying the beach, I was about to return to my hotel when suddenly it started raining heavily. I couldn't take a bus or a cab, there were no houses or people on the road, mountains on one side and the sea on the other. I was really in scared, thinking, "It's over," "Everything is over," and "There is no way to save this.

But suddenly I realized that I needed to stay calm and keep walking. Luckily, after about 20 minutes of walking in the rain, I met a kind family and they drove me to hotel. Through this experience, I realized that just thinking and not taking action is meaningless. Therefore, in order to overcome this weakness, from now on I must take action as soon as possible instead of thinking excessively.
