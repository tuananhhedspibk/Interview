# Experience

Align the perspective between the engineer and the business.
Engineers always want their systems to be designed with standards and their databases to be standard.
This ensures scalability and ease of maintenance, though,
The business side only recognizes the need to build this functionality into the product and provide it to the end user.

Therefore, how can we align the perspectives of both sides?

It is normal for misunderstandings and conflicts between engineers and business to arise in working, but in the end, both sides must work together to provide the best service to the users.

I believe that communication is the way to solve this problem
and through communicating we can understand each other (what is troubling or what are they thinking)
and after that we can find the most appropriate approach together.
The engineers should accept that a system that is against some standard design rules,
The business side can adjust their initial requirements to make it as easy as possible for the engineers to implement.

In conclusion
I have learned that teamwork and communication skills are very important for IT engineers, as well as IT knowledge.
