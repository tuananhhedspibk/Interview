# Improve your Japanese

I usually learn Japanese through real life experiences. For example, instead of hanging out with Vietnamese friends or going to Vietnamese restaurants,
I go alone to a place where there are many Japanese people, for example, a park, and there, I ask Japanese people for information about the park or a Japanese restaurant nearby,
Then, I would go to the restaurant and order food. Simply put, I learn Japanese like a tourist.

At work, I actively keep in touch and communicate with my Japanese colleagues.
I learn how to use words and imitate the way Japanese people speak, rather than translating from Japanese to Vietnamese.
In other words, it is the same image as Japanese children learning Japanese from their parents.
