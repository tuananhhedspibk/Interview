# Learn New Tech

I really enjoy exploring new technologies. In my opinion, each technology has its own merits and strengths. I find it gratifying to understand the essence, operation, and strengths of a particular technology. When delving into new technologies, I typically search for blogs that explain fundamental concepts and provide accompanying examples. I believe that reading about concepts alone can be challenging, and having practical examples enhances understanding and deepens knowledge of the technology.

After reading blogs, I like to undertake a small project applying the newly acquired technology. I believe that hands-on experience is the best way to solidify one's understanding of the fundamental aspects of a technology.
