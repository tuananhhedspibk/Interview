# Learn New Tech

I really like to self-study new technologies. To me, each technology has its own advantages and strengths.

I find it satisfying to understand the core logic of a particular technology. When studying a new technology,
I usually review the basic concepts, try to find blogs that provide examples to accompany them, and then imitate the blog examples.
The idea is to understand each concept. I believe that reading only the concepts is difficult to understand,
and through practical examples, we can understand the technology core logic more deeply.
