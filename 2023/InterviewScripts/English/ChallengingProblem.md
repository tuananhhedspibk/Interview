# Challenging problem

The project I'm working on at my current company is a "project related to payment", basically I have to touch the whole system,
So I'm a bit uncertain whether my design is really sufficient, and at that time I consult with the CTO and together we decide on an appropriate solution.
It's not only development, but I also do code reviews, so it's a lot of work.
To deal with that problem, I set my own concentration time up to twice a day and put it down,
If there is an urgent request, of course, I will respond to it. I work while checking the progress of the overall development and code review,
If the progress is not good, I will put the "requests that are not related to the project" in "Waiting" status during the concentration time (up to 30 - 45 minutes) for focusing on my own development and code review.
After the "Concentration Time" is over, I will do my best to digest all the "Waiting Requests" in one go.
