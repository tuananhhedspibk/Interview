# Change Job Reason

I have gained various experiences in my current company over the past three years, involving development, estimation, and even live coding. These experiences have provided valuable insights and a sense of personal growth. However, with a current Daily Active Users (DAU) of around 30,000, I feel that the scale is not as large as I would like it to be. Therefore, I am eager to experience working on a larger-scale system.

For instance, I am interested in facing challenges such as dealing with a broad impact during the development of new features or understanding the necessary elements to withstand a surge in visitors during peak times. I believe that encountering these challenges will contribute significantly to my growth.

Over the past year, I have felt that I haven't grown much. The lack of new challenges in my current job might be the reason for this. That's why I have decided to seek a new environment to test and push myself. Starting everything from scratch may be difficult, but I believe these challenges will contribute to my personal growth, and I prefer this over staying in a too comfortable and safe environment.

Furthermore, in my current company, there aren't many outstanding engineers. That's why I am looking to work in an environment with talented engineers. I am eager to learn from these skilled individuals, studying their experiences and problem-solving approaches.
