# Solving Conflict

I think it is normal for team members and leaders to have conflicts and misunderstandings because we are all human beings, but since team members and leaders belong to the same team, it is not about who is right or who wins, but about "solving team problems" and "achieving team goals," so we have to put our heads down and communicate with each other. Once we understand each other, we analyze the issues we are facing, and together we decide which priority should be given and what should be done in the interim to find the best way to address them.

In the most recent project, we decided on the overall architecture, but we had not yet decided on how to write the code and build the folder. I wanted to use the standard way of writing, but it took a lot of time and was difficult for other members to follow,
I worked with the leader to organize the way I proposed, and we were happy with the way we organized it, and finished development and testing two weeks before the deadline.

So I think communication is very important to resolve conflicts and contradictions.
