# Career Path

Long term, I would like to become a Tech Lead. This involves interacting with the business side and understanding the operations and requirements of the business. From there, I will model a domain model for the system, and on the technical side, I aim to have the ability to design the architecture of the system, design the modules, and determine how these modules will work together. They will also be involved in (and have a hand in) the design of the database and the selection of the technology to be used in the system.

On the technical side, I would like to have the ability to inspire and lead a team, as well as being technology savvy. I also want to develop my project management skills so that projects are implemented smoothly and conform to the client's needs.

To achieve these goals, I focus on improving my technical skills. In particular, in system architecture design, database management, and technology selection. We also build communication skills with stakeholders from the business side to the development team. I work hard to thoroughly understand the business requirements and to propose effective technical solutions to them.

I believe that the combination of my technical knowledge and my ability to communicate with the business side enhances my personal value and allows me to positively contribute to the development of the organization.
