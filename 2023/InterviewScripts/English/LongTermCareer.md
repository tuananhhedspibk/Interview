# Career Path

In the long term, I aim to become a tech lead, tasked with interacting with the business side to understand its operations and requirements. From there, I would model and create a domain model for the system. On the technical side, I aspire to be someone capable of designing the architecture of a system, designing modules, and determining how these modules interact with each other. Additionally, I would also be responsible for database design and choosing the technologies used in the system.

In the tech realm, I also want to become a leader in the technology industry, capable of motivating and guiding a team. I aim to develop project management skills to ensure that projects are efficiently implemented and meet the needs of the clients.

To achieve these goals, I am focusing on enhancing my technical skills, particularly in system architecture design, database management, and technology selection. I am also building communication and interaction skills with stakeholders, from the business side to the development team, to thoroughly understand business requirements and transform them into effective technological solutions.

I believe that the combination of solid technical knowledge and the ability to interact with the business side will enhance my personal value and positively contribute to the organization's development.
