# Constructive Feedback

I have done a mentoring job for a new graduate member. She didn't have very good coding skills
and kept repeating mistakes that I had warned her about many times.
It was pretty stressful during code reviews, and once time I have told her "Oh my God, I warned you so many times".
I talked to the team leader about it and the leader said, "we can't control my feelings, but we can control my actions.
Maybe if you say it in a gentler way, you might be able to get there.", so in the next coding review time I told her, "I'm sorry about the last time",
I told her that what we were doing was to contribute to the company's service, and that she should take responsibility for her work.
After two weeks, her code quality was getting better and better. When looking back at myself, I realize that
it's good to support the other person's growth, but the way we do it is much more important.
