I talked to the team leader about it in a 1-on-1 and the leader said, "I can't control my feelings, but I can control my actions.

I told her, "We're doing this to contribute to the company's service, so please take responsibility for what you're doing, and that's why I want you to remember the mistakes I warned you about from now on.
I told her, "Please don't repeat it," and after that her code quality was getting better and better. I looked back on it myself.
